Inheritance

Logline: When her sleepwalking nightmares uncover the dark and ancient secret of her family's lineage, Mallory must combat the inheritance before it reaches her brother.

Genre: Horror, Drama

Format: Short Film Script

Length: 15 pages

Synopsis: While Mallory navigates raising her teenage brother, Eli, she begins to have frightening sleepwalking episodes. Her episodes leak into the real world, scaring those around her but worse, jeopardizing the only stable adult Eli has. Her waking nightmares lead her to a terrible truth about her family's legacy and she must choose between protecting her brother or damning herself.

My Process

Inheritance is a script sample I wrote for a class assignment. I wanted to write a horror story that explored the ideas of family, folklore, and the things we inherit from our ancestors. I was inspired by the idea of an inherited evil as a metaphor for generational curses like mental illness, financial instability, and trauma. I also wanted to explore the relationship between siblings and how deep those bonds can be, especially when they are filling multiple roles in each other's lives.

The idea for this story came from my interest in my own culture and the stories passed down through my family. After researching the variations of the folklore associated with the Tlahuelpuchi, I became fascinated by the passing down of this curse through the women in a family. I considered what other curses are often passed down parent to child, particularly mother to daughter. In hispanic families, heavy responsibility to sacrifice and care for others is often placed on the shoulders of the daughters. Through this lens, I explored the idea of inheritance as both a curse and a duty.
