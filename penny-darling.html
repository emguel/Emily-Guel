Penny, Darling

Logline: A date with a creep leads Penny to discover a murderous side of herself. With this new side unlocked, Penny must find the will to control it or face the consequences.

Genre: Horror, Drama

Format: Short Film Sample

Length: 10 pages

Synopsis: While on the hunt for love, Penny encounters just the right guy. But when her date turns out to be a creep, Penny's night takes a turn for the worse. After a violent confrontation, Penny discovers a murderous side of herself she never knew existed. As she tries to control this new side of herself, Penny's life spirals out of control. She can't stand the objectification of herself anymore, especially now that she knows there's another option. This relief is short lived as a detective soon goes snooping around her trail of splattered hearts.

My Process

Penny, Darling is a script sample I wrote after contemplating the dangers of online dating. I wanted to write a horror story that explored power dynamics in dating and the frustrations that come with it for women. The idea for Penny came to me after a drive home from classes. Psycho Killer by Talking Heads played on the radio and the catchy song followed me inside my house. I sat on my couch, bored and began swiping on dating apps. I matched with a guy and almost immediately, he invited me over to his house. I thought, aren't guys taught to never let strangers into their homes? Possibly not. So, I asked him "What if I'm a serial killer?" and he thought it was so charming, that he invited me to a concert that night. I deleted the app, and started writing.

I was so fascinated by the lack of fear the men I spoke to had. It became almost maddening that it wasn't just a lack of understanding about the dangers, but they truly believed women were no threat to them. I continued with that idea, and thought maybe they just hadn't met the right woman yet. Through Penny, I explored a reversal of the underlying and dangerous dynamics between women and men in the dating scene.
